The design problem

An AI-native audit platform that keeps auditors accountable. Agents do the research and analysis. Auditors stay accountable for the conclusion.

The design problem is the boundary between agent and auditor: what the agent may access, where a human must review, how the auditor sees what the agent did and what the system does when the agent is wrong. Those four questions run under every artifact in this case. This artifact fixes the constraints each one answers under.

I framed the problem this way at the start of discovery, before the research in 02 and 03. The research tested the framing rather than produced it. Where a constraint below was sharpened by a finding, the evidence table in 03 says so.

I decided: to frame the problem as the boundary between agent and auditor, not as adding AI to the workpaper. The triad table as the shared artifact PMs and engineering leads plan against. We built: the constraint list with methodology reviewers, the engagement data lead role and engineering leads. The data classes in C3 were drafted with the data lead. Design team of three at this phase.

The user problem

Four problems as framed at discovery start. Each has a mechanism, not an adjective. Validated against the synthesis in 03.

ProblemMechanism
Fragmented toolsEvidence, workpapers, client requests and review live in separate systems. The auditor reassembles the picture by hand for every sample.
Manual evidence reviewEach sampled item is traced to its documents one at a time. The work is repetitive and the volume peaks when time is shortest.
Inconsistent workpapersEach team formats workpapers its own way. Reviewers decode the format before they can judge the substance.
Deadline pressureBusy season concentrates all of the above into a few weeks. Shortcuts taken under pressure are the quality risk the platform exists to remove.

What this framing rules out

A chat assistant beside the workpaper. It answers questions but leaves C1, C2 and C4 untouched: the record is a transcript, the auditor cannot show what was read and review has nowhere to live. An agent that writes directly into workpaper cells. It violates C2 and hides the steps C4 requires. Both directions were sketched and rejected in 07; the rejection reasons are these constraints.

What it rules in: explicit agent runs with recorded steps, a permission model the auditor can see, review as a destination and sign-off as a human-only control. That is D-01. The data classes in C3 are D-05.
