What these maps are

One Test of Details on the revenue area, mapped three ways. The current-state journey follows the Senior through nine lifecycle stages with what she does, thinks and feels, where it hurts and where the opportunity is. The service blueprint shows what happens behind her: client requests, data pulls and the systems that carry the work, including the shadow spreadsheet. The future-state journey puts agents in the lane and names the human checkpoint at each stage, so the delta is visible stage by stage.

Reconstructed from the themes in 03. Emotion values are the lead's reading of the evidence on a five-point scale, not a measurement. Future-state feeling is design intent.

I decided To map one Test of Details through the Senior's eyes, to put the emotion curve where the deadline bites and to place the mandatory checkpoints where judgment lives. Where the checkpoints went and why

Mandatory checkpoints sit at four places: plan approval before a run starts, any retrieval of restricted-class material, every exception resolution and the moment a draft becomes the conclusion. Sign-off stays human-only by construction. The other stages have no mandatory checkpoint because the research put judgment at exceptions and conclusions, not at matching. A low-confidence match routes to review on its own. That is D-09: checkpoints where judgment lives, not everywhere and not nowhere.
